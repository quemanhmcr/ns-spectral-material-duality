# High-companion recurrence reduces to fresh HH/relink/source or high strain

Status: **OWNER REDUCTION / OPEN FRESH-OWNER EXHAUSTION**.  This note updates `U` after the radial-memory and low-strain carrier theorems.

## 1. The old formulation of U was too broad

The previous graph kept

\[
U=\text{repeated high-companion/new-ancestry funding}
\]

because a low donor cannot transfer energy to a lone UV recipient: closed-triad geometry forces a high companion at the same event.

But several physically different situations were hidden under the word “companion.”

## 2. Passive old high stock is no longer an escape

Theorem BK shows that old high-tail modal stock is exponentially erased unless actual energy crosses into the high set.

Theorem BO is stronger on a selected smooth carrier: even after allowing the exact symmetric resolved strain multiplier, a critical terminal carrier cannot persist through `O((log N)/(nu N^2))` while both

- strain action stays below its physical face, and
- HH/relink/source input stays absent.

Thus an old companion is not a free persistent catalyst.

## 3. Low--high incidence has only typed exits

Current Wang smooth `Q^2` energy calculus and resolved-interface quotient already give the native split after common observer transport is removed:

\[
\text{resolved incidence}
\to
\begin{cases}
\text{skew conservative donor/relink},\\
\text{symmetric strain/deformation}.
\end{cases}
\]

The skew branch is same-event provenance and has zero recursion depth; the symmetric branch is the existing strain owner.  There is no third low--high energy currency.

Therefore nonlocal low-donor/high-companion transfer does not by itself create a new recursive class.  It either traces existing high stock by relink or fires strain.

## 4. What genuinely remains of U

After this quotient, an infinite UV recurrence can survive only through repeated **fresh-owner renewal**:

1. actual positive HH generation at high scale;
2. relink to a different high stock whose own ancestry must then be traced;
3. source/material renewal of that high stock;
4. high strain, which breaks any low-strain epoch and enters its own dissipation/descending-ancestor law.

Pure eventually-high-strain recurrence and pure signed-good generated-HH recurrence are already finite in current Wang.  Hence the remaining unresolved core is their **mixed/generic renewal**, especially generic HH work that has not acquired signed-good scale geometry and relink/source changes that can renew the relevant high state.

## 5. Updated graph after S removal

The earlier `U/R` graph can now be written more sharply as

\[
\boxed{
G/R
}
\]

where

- `G` = **generic fresh high-frequency generation/relink/source recurrence** after low-strain passive memory has been removed;

This renaming is not a theorem that `G` is one scalar owner.  It is a statement that the old-companion mechanism itself has been exhausted; only fresh typed owner events remain.

## 6. Next exact target

The next useful theorem should not estimate gross HH work.  It should disintegrate the fresh `G` law by **radial provenance**:

- comparable/local HH generation;
- resolved low--high relink/strain (already typed);
- source/material renewal;
- any higher-state donor already present.

Then one can ask whether repeated fresh `G` events either enter the already-finite signed-good/high-strain epochs or force a state-renewal action in `R`.

No recurrence termination or global-regularity theorem is claimed.
