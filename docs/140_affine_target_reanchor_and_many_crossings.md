## 4. Exact heat shears can realize arbitrarily many prescribed transverse ranking crossings with zero nonlinear interaction

This strengthens the single-crossing witness of `docs/139_enstrophy_ranking_crossing_event_typing.md`.

Fix any finite ordered times

\[
0<t_1<\cdots<t_N,
\]

and choose `N+1` distinct odd Fourier integers

\[
n_0,\ldots,n_N.
\]

The functions

\[
e^{-\nu n_j^2t}
\]

form a Chebyshev system.  The `N x (N+1)` homogeneous interpolation problem

\[
\sum_{j=0}^N a_j e^{-\nu n_j^2t_i}=0,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,N,
\]

has a nonzero solution `a`.  Define

\[
O(t)=\sum_{j=0}^N a_j e^{-\nu n_j^2t}.
\]

A nonzero exponential polynomial with `N+1` distinct exponents has at most `N` zeros counting multiplicity.  Since `O` already has the `N` prescribed distinct zeros, every one is simple:

\[
\boxed{O(t_i)=0,\qquad O'(t_i)\ne0.}
\]

Now take `B>0`, `epsilon>0`,

\[
E(t)=B e^{-4\nu t},
\]

and the periodic shear

\[
\boxed{
U(y,t)=
-\frac{B}{2}e^{-4\nu t}\sin2y
-\varepsilon\sum_{j=0}^N\frac{a_j}{n_j}e^{-\nu n_j^2t}\sin(n_jy).}
\]

Every Fourier mode solves the heat equation and the velocity has only an x-component independent of x, hence

\[
\boxed{U_t=\nu U_{yy},\qquad (u\cdot\nabla)u=0.}
\]

Its scalar vorticity amplitude is

\[
w=-U_y
=E(t)\cos2y
+\varepsilon\sum_j a_j e^{-\nu n_j^2t}\cos(n_jy).
\]

At the fixed critical sheets `y=0` and `y=pi`, oddness of every `n_j` gives

\[
w_0=E+\varepsilon O,
\qquad
w_\pi=E-\varepsilon O.
\]

Therefore their enstrophy-value gap is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\Delta e(t)
=\frac12(w_0^2-w_\pi^2)
=2\varepsilon E(t)O(t).}
\]

Hence every prescribed `t_i` is a transverse ranking crossing:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta e(t_i)=0,
\qquad
\dot{\Delta e}(t_i)=2\varepsilon E(t_i)O'(t_i)\ne0.}
\]

The sheets are critical for every time because `w_y=0` at `0,pi`.

They can moreover be kept as strict transverse enstrophy maxima at every prescribed crossing.  Put

\[
O_2(t)=\sum_j n_j^2a_j e^{-\nu n_j^2t}.
\]

At a crossing,

\[
e_{yy}(0,t_i)=E_i(-4E_i-\varepsilon O_{2,i}),
\]

\[
e_{yy}(\pi,t_i)=E_i(-4E_i+\varepsilon O_{2,i}).
\]

Because there are only finitely many `t_i` and every `E_i>0`, choose `epsilon` so small that

\[
\varepsilon\max_i|O_{2,i}|<4\min_iE_i.
\]

Then both transverse curvatures are strictly negative at every crossing.

Thus for **every finite N** there is an exact smooth periodic Navier--Stokes shear with at least `N` prescribed, transverse, strict-critical-sheet ranking crossings and identically zero nonlinear advection.
