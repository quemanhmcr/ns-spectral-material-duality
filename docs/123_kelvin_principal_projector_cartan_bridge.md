## 1. Principal metric projectors are connection-comoving

Let the positive line metric have simple spectrum

\[

\qquad
\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3),
\]

\[
P_i=v_iv_i^T.
\]

Define the moving-eigenframe connection

\[
\boxed{C_M:=\dot V V^T,\qquad C_M^T=-C_M.}
\]

Then directly

\[
\boxed{\dot P_i=[C_M,P_i].}
\]

Therefore every principal projector has zero repo-3 Cartan defect relative to its **own physical metric-eigenframe connection**:

\[
\boxed{G_{P_i}^{M}:=\dot P_i-[C_M,P_i]=0.}
\]

The principal-axis traffic is thus moving-coordinate transport, not selector production.

\[

\[
