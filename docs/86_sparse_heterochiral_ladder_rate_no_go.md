# A sparse heterochiral Fourier ladder can be born but cannot carry the fixed-mass rate gate at ultraviolet scales

Status: **COUNTEREXAMPLE/NO-GO TO SPARSE-LADDER COMPLETION**.

CK shows an exact Hadamard geometry with fixed finite branching width.  Its importance is adversarial: topology and phase birth may permit several generations.  CL shows why that does not yet threaten regularity at the critical record rate.

If a ladder uses at most `M_0` effective Fourier/helicity modes at each scale, then

\[
M_{eff}\le M_0.
\]

At fixed critical mass `mu~nu^2`, CL requires

\[
M_0\gtrsim N^3.
\]

This fails for all sufficiently large `N`.

Thus a sparse self-similar triad chain is not the missing Navier--Stokes blow-up mechanism even if an actual Galerkin referee shows nonzero multigeneration birth.  To remain rate critical it must broaden to `O(N^3)` effective Fourier participation, or increase its critical mass sufficiently to leave the fixed-crossing regime.  Both are separately typed physical alternatives.

The theorem is not a statement that broad Fourier participation itself is impossible.  It removes a misleading finite-mode picture and identifies the next real question: can NSE maintain **dense coherent heterochiral pair production** across the critical front?
