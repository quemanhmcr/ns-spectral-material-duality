# A nonlocal upward energy jump requires a pre-existing comparable high-frequency companion

Status: **EXACT CLOSED-TRIAD GEOMETRY / RIGOROUS OWNER REDUCTION**.

The radial energy lineage can make a large same-time jump only through an actual quadratic Navier--Stokes triad.  The third mode in that interaction prevents a low-frequency donor from creating an isolated ultraviolet recipient from nothing.

## 1. Closed-triad geometry

Let three physical Fourier wavevectors satisfy

\[
k_d+k_c+k_r=0,
\]

where `d` is one energy-donor root and `r` one recipient root in the actual same-time cyclic transport.  The remaining triad root is `c`.

Then

\[
\boxed{
|k_c|=|k_d+k_r|
\ge\bigl||k_r|-|k_d|\bigr|.
}
\]

Suppose the recipient is nonlocally higher than this donor:

\[
|k_r|\ge\Lambda |k_d|,
\qquad \Lambda>1.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
|k_c|
\ge\left(1-\frac1\Lambda\right)|k_r|.
}
\]

Thus a large donor-to-recipient frequency jump requires another physical mode already comparable to the recipient scale at the same event.

The companion may itself be a second energy donor in a two-donor/one-recipient sign pattern.  The statement does not impose a unique catalyst ontology; it only records unavoidable high-frequency support in the actual closed triad.

## 2. Parabolic corridor-skipping specialization

At fixed physical time let

\[
a_j=2\nu(T-t)|k_j|^2.
\]

If an energy donor lies below the lower parabolic face

\[
a_d<\alpha
\]

while its recipient lies above the upper face

\[
a_r>\beta,
\qquad \beta>\alpha,
\]

then

\[
\frac{|k_r|}{|k_d|}>\sqrt{\frac\beta\alpha}.
\]

Hence the third root satisfies

\[
\boxed{
|k_c|
>
\left(1-\sqrt{\frac\alpha\beta}\right)|k_r|.
}
\]

Therefore a one-event subparabolic-to-superparabolic skip necessarily uses a contemporaneous high-frequency companion.

## 3. Owner consequence

A superparabolic recipient reached by a nonlocal energy jump has only two possibilities for its comparable high-frequency companion:

1. **old/materially reused companion**: then its ability to service progressively higher generations is subject to the catalyst half-life / strain geometry of Theorem BB;

If high strain allows an old companion to track the advancing frequency, high strain itself has fired as a separately typed owner.

Thus the high-tail corridor-skipping branch is not an independent “teleportation” mechanism.  It reduces to high-frequency companion ancestry plus the actual donor-energy transfer.

## 4. What the theorem does not say

It does not give a lower bound on the companion amplitude.  A very small high-frequency companion may interact with a large other role; multiplicative amplitude productivity / work-weighted breadth remains relevant.  It also does not identify the companion with Kelvin material ancestry automatically.

The remaining amplitude/ancestry problem is explicit rather than hidden inside the nonlocal jump.
