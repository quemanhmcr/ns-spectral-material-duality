## 1. The unresolved field is itself a legitimate transporter

Let `h` be any smooth divergence-free unresolved field and define

\[
\mathcal B(h,h)=\mathbb P[(h\cdot\nabla)h].
\]

The linearized operator about `h` is

\[
\mathcal L_hf
=\mathbb P[(h\cdot\nabla)f+(f\cdot\nabla)h].
\]

Therefore, simply by bilinearity,

\[
\boxed{\mathcal L_hh=2\mathcal B(h,h).}
\]

By Theorem CW,

\[
\mathcal L_h=\mathcal K_h+\mathcal S_h,
\]

where `K_h` is skew-adjoint and `S_h` is the symmetric strain operator generated by

\[
S_h=\operatorname{sym}\nabla h.
\]

## 2. Exact complete-role HH identity

Take any finite complete orthogonal role decomposition

\[
h=\sum_bw_b.
\]

Define the signed HH child work into role `a`

\[
T_a^{HH}:=\langle w_a,\mathcal B(h,h)\rangle.
\]

Define unresolved Cartan role matrices

\[
K^{(h)}_{ab}=\langle w_a,\mathcal K_hw_b\rangle,
\qquad
S^{(h)}_{ab}=\langle w_a,\mathcal S_hw_b\rangle.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
T_a^{HH}
=\frac12\sum_b\left(K^{(h)}_{ab}+S^{(h)}_{ab}\right).
}
\]

Thus actual HH work has two simultaneous geometric readings:

1. canonical helical/triad donor-recipient work;
2. row divergence of an unresolved connection 2-form plus unresolved material metric-velocity form.

Neither reading replaces the other.

## 3. Global energy conservation appears automatically

Summing all child roles,

\[
\sum_aT_a^{HH}
=\langle h,\mathcal B(h,h)\rangle=0.
\]

The Cartan form reproduces this in two pieces.  Since `K^(h)` is skew,

\[
\mathbf 1^TK^{(h)}\mathbf 1=0.
\]

And because

\[
\langle h,\mathcal S_hh\rangle
=\langle h,\mathcal L_hh\rangle

\[
\boxed{\mathbf 1^TS^{(h)}\mathbf 1=0.}
\]

This does **not** say the symmetric matrix vanishes.  Its diagonal/off-diagonal role entries can be large and signed while the complete-field self-work cancels.

\[
2T_a^{HH}
=\sum_bK^{(h)}_{ab}+\sum_bS^{(h)}_{ab}.

\[

This protects Wang's canonical positive HH cause from being silently replaced by a material-strain positive part.  The material bridge is tensorial and signed.
