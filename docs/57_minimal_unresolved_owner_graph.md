# Minimal unresolved owner graph after the killed-lineage/radial-current reduction

Status: **STRUCTURAL PROOF REDUCTION / OPEN BRIDGE**.  This note deliberately contains no new regularity claim.  Its purpose is to remove mechanisms that no longer deserve independent nodes in the global recurrence graph.

## 1. Root of every candidate singular path: actual outward radial energy current

If a finite first singular time `T` exists, `H^1` enstrophy is unbounded and has arbitrarily late record-growth times.  At each such time Theorems BC/BH give

\[
\int_0^\infty\Phi_\uparrow(R)dR
\ge
\nu\|\Delta u\|_2^2
+
\int_0^\infty\Phi_\downarrow(R)dR.
\]

Therefore every candidate singular mechanism must supply actual outward kinetic-energy current across radial Fourier boundaries.  This is the root node of the graph.

Microscopic phase, strain, material geometry and stochastic state structure matter only insofar as they determine or reroute this actual physical current or its ancestry.  They are not extra sources of kinetic energy.

## 2. Mechanisms that no longer need independent recurrence nodes

### Pressure / Bernoulli gauge
Exact Leray/curl/closed-current gauge.  No energy or circulation source.

### Common incompressible deformation
Phase-neutral on the common cubic and conservative at energy level.  Relative deformation can change interactions, but the resulting actual work is already read through `K`.

### Phase as an independent global currency
Phase controls sign/efficiency of microscopic work.  Once actual signed work has been registered into `K`, global enstrophy growth reads `K` directly.  Dephasing lowers/reroutes physical transfer; persistent favorable phase must still pass the same energy-lineage killing/current laws.  The phase ledger remains a local no-hidden-escape theorem, not a separate global stock.

### Covariance / q.v. as an independent energy source
Kelvin q.v., deformation covariance, resolution covariance and their new full-current cross covariance are distinct exact state-resolution objects.  None appears as an additional deterministic kinetic-energy source in AR/BC.  Their global role is state/selection/restart fidelity, not creation of energy stock.

### Hard cells / checkpoints / observer rereading
Current Wang mode-set theorem confirms persistent stock is modal energy, not hard interaction cells.  Same-carrier checkpoints and coarse self-loops are representation/state observations, not fresh energy ancestry.

### Eventually pure high-strain recurrence
Current Wang descending high-strain epochs are finite on their own native physical gradient reservoir.  High strain can still alternate with other owners, but it is not an independent infinite tail.

### Eventually pure signed-good generated-HH recurrence
Current Wang parabolic registration geometry makes consecutive signed-good generated-HH depth finite.  Generic/nonlocal HH remains only through the residual graph below.

### One old matched-corridor energy population
BF excludes it from carrying the terminal `H^1` blow-up rate.  Persistent corridor activity needs arbitrarily late physical input.

### One old low-strain material catalyst
BB gives geometric service half-life against an advancing scale.  Infinite efficient reuse forces strain, relink, fragmentation or service failure.

## 3. Three unresolved recurrence seams

### S — Subparabolic critical-shell renewal
The unique heat currency becomes cheap when

\[
a=2\nu(T-t)N^2\ll1.
\]

BJ gives a concrete PDE-derived highest critical shell at every enstrophy record and makes all higher shells critical-subthreshold until the next crossing.  What is still missing is a full-PDE theorem saying that such a subparabolic critical state either:

1. has an own-scale continuation interval extending beyond the candidate singular time; or
2. forces a named low-frequency strain/catalyst/source/interface owner before that interval ends.

This is the sharp version of the old vague “define bad_flags” problem.

### U — High-companion renewal in superparabolic/nonlocal transfer
A corridor-skipping donor jump cannot create a lone UV recipient: BI forces a comparable high-frequency companion in the same closed triad.  If the companion is materially old and low-strain, BB erodes its service.  If strain tracks it upward, the strain owner fires.  Therefore indefinite nonlocal UV transfer can survive only by repeatedly supplying **new/relinked/fragmented high-frequency companion ancestry** or by alternating with a genuine high-tail/source mechanism.

The unresolved theorem is a work-weighted exhaustion of this companion renewal, not a bound on one gross transfer scalar.

### R — Material/reselection reentry without a physical gap or single-charge descent
Modal energy itself has global ancestry and is not cloned by nonlinearity.  But a selected material/current state can be changed by relink, finite-shape refinement, replica-dependent reselection or reduction.  Kelvin `7dc3a87` now gives exact full current-shape covariance and deformation--circulation cross terms, while explicitly retaining finite relative shape as a separate first-order state.

If actual bad/resolve semantics supplies a physical hysteresis gap, BG prices repeated parabolic reentry by viscous killing unless a reverse owner fires.  Without such a gap, or without kernel-pure/single-charge state descent, grazing/relabelled reentry is still open.

## 4. Breadth is not a fourth energy source

Amplitude entropy correctly shows that a fixed mass floor cannot be imposed on every structural parent.  This creates a breadth/fragmentation issue, but not a new kinetic-energy source: all descendants are still funded through the same finite modal energy stock and donor current.

Breadth becomes dangerous only insofar as it can continually renew the **companion/material ancestry** in U or R while keeping record outward radial current alive.  Thus work-weighted entropy/packing belongs inside the U/R exhaustion theorem rather than beside kinetic energy as an unrelated master currency.

## 5. The resulting short proof skeleton

A future full proof need not replay the entire discovery spine.  It could have the following form:

1. **Record-current theorem:** finite singular time -> arbitrarily late outward radial energy current (BC/BH + standard `H^1` restart).
2. **Unique parabolic decomposition:** the exact energy/clock/viscosity generator forces sub/matched/super regions (BD).
3. **Matched termination:** old matched populations die too fast; forward/reentry progress pays killing (AT, BF, BG).
4. **Subparabolic first-bad theorem:** close S by deriving full-PDE own-scale continuation or a named owner.
5. **Companion/material exhaustion theorem:** close U/R using triad companion geometry, catalyst half-life, strain/high-tail ownership, state purity and work-weighted fragmentation/reuse.
6. **Assembly:** with S/U/R closed, no arbitrarily late record-current path remains, contradicting the first singular time.

Only steps 4--6 are genuinely missing at programme level; step 5 may itself split into a few lemmas.

## 6. Strong warning on scope

This reduction is not a proof that every Wang/Kelvin owner theorem automatically applies to one common state.  Repo 3 continues to use upstream results only through their literal typed statements.  In particular:

- Wang modal energy ancestry is not Kelvin material ancestry;
- Kelvin current-shape covariance is not modal energy stock;
- entropy/reuse weights are not heat-killing weights;
- high-tail or strain owner labels do not acquire global termination merely by being named.

The value of the graph is exactly that these non-equivalences have already been audited, leaving a small number of explicit interfaces rather than an artificial merged architecture.

## 7. Downstream refinement: S has now been routed into actual input work

Theorems BK--BM sharpen Section 3.  Radial high-set continuity shows that any late critical shell loses old UV memory exponentially and therefore requires recent actual upward radial funding.  Independently, the higher tail above the highest PDE-active shell is small enough in the critical amplitude that its pure self-interaction is viscosity-absorbable; any tail record growth forces lower-frequency incidence/boundary work.

Consequently `S` is no longer retained as an **independent** recurrence owner.  Its two possibilities are now:

- a higher critical crossing, which is an actual radial-work event and enters the existing edge/companion/interface routing;
- no higher crossing, in which case unbounded subcritical-tail growth forces an external incidence owner.

The minimal unresolved owner graph after this refinement is therefore

\[
\boxed{U/R}
\]

plus the final measurable assembly problem.  This is an owner reduction, not a claim that `U` or `R` has terminated and not a Navier--Stokes regularity theorem.
