# Research programme

## Central question

Can one derive a representation-independent geometric spine directly from incompressible Navier–Stokes and then recover both spectral/helical and material/Kelvin observables as coordinate realizations of that spine?

The intended logical direction is

\[
\mathrm{NSE}\to\text{common intrinsic geometry}
\to
\begin{cases}
\text{spectral/helical observables},\\
\text{material/Kelvin observables}.
\end{cases}
\]

We do not infer a bridge merely because formulas in two repositories look similar.

## What success would mean

A useful theorem from this programme should do at least one of the following:

1. translate a certified obstruction/resource in one representation into a certified obstruction/resource in the other;
2. prove an invariant that removes duplicated proof work;
3. identify a hidden variable that one representation was suppressing;
4. produce an explicit counterexample to a tempting but false identification;
5. reveal a third coercive quantity natural to the PDE itself.
