# Paper outline — Spectral–material duality in 3D Navier–Stokes

Working title:

**Dual deformation geometry for spectral triads and material Kelvin packets in three-dimensional Navier–Stokes**

## Abstract skeleton

We study two coordinate descriptions of incompressible Navier–Stokes deformation: Fourier/helical triads and material/Kelvin vorticity-flux packets. Starting directly from the PDE and flow-map kinematics, we identify a common deformation geometry generated by `F`, with material metric `M=F^T F` and spectral covector metric `M^{-1}`. We derive exact dictionaries for advected wavefront/material-surface normals, objective strain, metric acceleration, near-extremal triad shape, and noncommuting-strain holonomy. We then establish a sharp structural boundary: material metric velocity determines local helicity conversion, but material metric geometry alone cannot determine signed nonlinear Fourier edge work because relative complex phase remains an independent physical variable. This yields both bridge theorems and no-go theorems, clarifying which quantities may be transferred between Eulerian spectral and Lagrangian material descriptions without conflating geometry, capacity, covariance, or causal work.
