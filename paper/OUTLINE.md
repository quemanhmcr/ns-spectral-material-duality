# Paper outline — Spectral–material duality in 3D Navier–Stokes

Working title:

**Dual deformation geometry for spectral triads and material Kelvin packets in three-dimensional Navier–Stokes**

## Abstract skeleton

We study two coordinate descriptions of incompressible Navier–Stokes deformation: Fourier/helical triads and material/Kelvin vorticity-flux packets. Starting directly from the PDE and flow-map kinematics, we identify a common deformation geometry generated by `F`, with material metric `M=F^T F` and spectral covector metric `M^{-1}`. We derive exact dictionaries for advected wavefront/material-surface normals, objective strain, metric acceleration, near-extremal triad shape, and noncommuting-strain holonomy. We then identify the missing signed-interaction object: a complex, `GL(3)`-invariant oriented material vorticity-flux 3-form whose real part reproduces signed helical edge work and whose argument is the gauge-invariant interaction phase.  The 3-form has a literal small-Kelvin-loop circulation realization.  Localizing the vorticity flux yields an exact source law containing only moving/interface transport, strain--selection mismatch, and viscosity after common Nanson deformation is removed; moving cuts carry an unavoidable time face.  Finally, monochromatic viscosity is shown to damp the interaction 3-form without rotating its phase.  These results supply both bridge and no-go theorems while keeping geometry, covariance, signed work, and recurrence logically distinct.
